Our vision

Taking on manual operations that are hard to automate

Flexible objects such as cables change shape with grasping and contact. People assess their condition and adjust force and sequence as they work. A robot needs to check that condition, adapt its motion and connect one operation to the next.

RLR Kashiwanoha focuses on operations that are hard to automate, such as cable routing and installation. Our research addresses the sequence from regrasping and support transfer through routing, fastening, terminal connection, completion checks and recovery.

The future we are working toward

We aim for production lines where previous development work helps make automation accessible as products and operating conditions change. Two priorities guide that work.

  1. 01

    Develop control that responds to the situation

    We research control that combines learning from example motions, adapting to the current state and connecting to the next operation. Individual motions and complete sequences each require validation.

    Learning and adapting motions: GQARL →Selecting and connecting operations: WMSO →
  2. 02

    Include safety, quality and recovery in the process

    Conditions for proceeding and stopping, human intervention and recovery are part of the process. We clarify the roles of equipment and control, and build the evidence needed before moving to physical application.

From simulation toward the factory floor

Current work covers simulation models, control development, training where needed and conditional validation for agreed processes. We also define requirements, equipment configurations, equipment and fixture specifications, measurement/control interfaces and test plans for physical validation. Specification work can be commissioned on its own, with evidence, assumptions, open issues and the owners responsible for confirming them documented.

We plan to develop Real2Sim, which brings information from physical equipment into models, and Sim2Real, which connects control to that equipment. By defining responsibilities with system integrators and equipment manufacturers, we aim to expand into site deployment and ongoing software support. Physical and production performance remain to be validated.
